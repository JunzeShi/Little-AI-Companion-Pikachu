A LITTLE HELP

Tips & capabilities

Ask naturally, or use the controls in the chat.

  • Chat locally. Start a local model in Bionic or LM Studio; choose it in Settings. Your messages and saved chats stay on this Mac.
  • Ask about a file. Drop a file onto the window, or use ＋ → Add a file. Supports PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), PowerPoint (.ppt/.pptx), Excel (.xlsx), RTF, text, Markdown, CSV, TSV and common images up to 20 MB. Questions use local excerpts. Images need a vision-capable model for visual details.
  • Document limits. PDFs examine up to 200 pages and attempt on-device text recognition on up to 50 scanned pages; results depend on legibility. PowerPoint extracts slide text, not embedded images or charts; older .ppt slide boundaries may be incomplete. Excel uses stored cell values and may show formulas without cached results; it does not recalculate or interpret charts and formatting. Long documents use selected excerpts.
  • Search a folder. Use ＋ → Choose a folder, then ask about its documents. Local keyword search checks up to 200 supported files of at most 2 MB each; images and scanned pages are skipped. Attach a scanned PDF directly to try text recognition. Clear removes the selection.
  • Look at my screen. Choose a display or selected area, or click Choose app/window to list open applications and windows. Choose a source, click the screen button, then ask your question. The button stays highlighted while the capture is ready; click it again or Clear to discard it. The capture is used once and then discarded. macOS may request Screen Recording permission.
  • Stop and moods. Stop a reply while Pikachu is working; the visible mood reflects your interactions and the result.
  • Chat memory. Older turns are summarized locally when a conversation approaches its context limit; the full chat remains in history.
  • Timers and stopwatch. Ask “set a timer for an hour” or “start a stopwatch.” Live clocks appear in Timer; cancel individual timers or pause and reset the stopwatch. macOS notifications can alert you when a timer ends.
  • Pomodoro. Open Pomodoro to set focus and break lengths, or ask “start a 25 minute Pomodoro.” Start, pause or reset; Pikachu gives a gentle reminder when each phase finishes. Start each break when you’re ready.
  • Pet routines. Pikachu can greet you in the morning and celebrate completed timers or an “I get it” moment. Use Celebrate for a cheer anytime; choose the fireworks and routine settings in Pet settings.
  • Math and translation. Ask a calculation or say “translate this into French.” Pikachu handles these without extra buttons. Equations are displayed with readable math symbols.
  • Web search. Click the web search button to cycle through Offline, Auto, and Always. Web searches send a query to the provider. Selected file, folder, and screen contents stay local.
  • Dictate. Choose a language and press the microphone. Review the draft before sending. Network speech is off unless you enable it in Settings.
  • Chat history. Open the history icon to start, revisit, archive, or delete chats. Settings let you change the pet’s name, model, voice privacy, and search connection.

File and folder selections are temporary; questions and answers are saved in your local chat history. Capture and file interpretation depend on the selected model.
